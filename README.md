# Ember Court

An original duel card game for the make.fun best-card-game bounty. Two players,
twenty life each, one card a round — and a Court Suit that doubles its cards.

## How to play

- Each side holds 5 cards from a 40-card deck: four suits (Flame, Tide, Thorn,
  Stone), values 1–9, plus one Court card (10) per suit.
- Every round a **Court Suit** is revealed. Cards of that suit fight at double
  value. Both sides play one card face down, then reveal.
- Higher value wins the trick and deals damage equal to the difference. First
  to 0 life loses.
- Court cards carry powers: **Flame** +4 damage, **Tide** heal 4, **Thorn**
  draw 2, **Stone** block the next damage.
- The ember-keeper's favoured suit glows and it muses aloud — read the tell,
  then counter it.

Click a card (or press 1–5) to play it. M toggles sound. No network, no
libraries: static files under `game/`, served by any static HTTP server.
